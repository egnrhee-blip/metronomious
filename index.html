Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Metronomious does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Ever.

What we collect

Nothing. Metronomious has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Local storage

Your setlists, presets, and settings are saved locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Third-party services

Metronomious does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or data collection services.

Permissions

Metronomious requests audio background mode so the metronome can continue playing when your screen is locked. No audio is recorded or transmitted — this permission is used solely to keep the metronome running.

Children

Metronomious does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
